Privacy Policy

Orb Rush: Neon Runner by Quick Tap Games. Effective October 26, 2026.

Summary

Orb Rush: Neon Runner has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your progress is saved on your device. The game uses third-party services for purchases and for optional rewarded ads, described below.

What stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked skins, settings and best scores are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to validate purchases and restore skins you own. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, and basic device and store information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Rewarded ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners. When an ad is requested or shown, these services may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, your IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. The game asks for your permission before any tracking happens through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the game works exactly the same and ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See Unity's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for personal information.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use the Google advertising products.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
