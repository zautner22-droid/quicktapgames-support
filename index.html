Support from Quick Tap Games

Need help? Email quicktapgamesofficial@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Restoring purchases

Skins you bought are tied to your Apple ID. If you reinstall the game or switch to a new iPhone, open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID. Coin packs are consumable, so they cannot be restored once spent.

A purchase did not arrive

Close and reopen the game with an internet connection and check the Store again. If the coins or skin still are not there, email us with the date of the purchase and the Apple receipt (from your email or Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions & Purchases > Purchase history).

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Rewarded ads

Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus or a second chance. If an ad does not load, try again in a few minutes. The game is fully playable without watching any ads.
